I literally & figuratively put out fires

I’ve spent most of my life fighting fires. For 15 years that meant real ones, as a volunteer firefighter; for even longer, it’s meant the digital kind. I’ve worked with names like Yahoo! and IBM and with hundreds of startups, and I still love the hard problems most people would rather avoid.
